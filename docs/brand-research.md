## Verified positioning

Quick GO is a microfranchise network using automated cocktail preparation. The official website describes applications in events, bars, restaurants, hotels and brand activations. Its mission combines access to premium cocktails with technology. It lists Mini GO and Tech GO models and describes training and franchisee support.

## Decisions for this site

- Keep this landing page focused on customers planning events.
- Route franchise enquiries to the official website's existing contact section, rather than inventing a separate franchise form.
- Use the official Instagram supplied by the user. Do not invent other social accounts, addresses, email addresses, nationwide service coverage or unit phone numbers.
- Do not repeat investment or profitability promises, response-time guarantees or unverified price figures.
- The newly supplied footer reference is adapted into four columns, with working event, network and social links. Replace its example newsletter with the real event quote entry point; there is no newsletter service configured.
- The footer appearance switch affects only the footer. It does not alter the hero or drink carousel.
- Only Anek Devanagari is currently installed from the manual. Hatton and Brixton Sans remain pending original font files.
- Official product cutouts were reviewed as equipment references. Existing supplied event imagery is better suited to the current editorial sections and remains in place.
